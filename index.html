About NovaSMP

NovaSMP is a survival‑multiplayer server that blends classic Minecraft mechanics with custom plugins for a fresh experience. Join a friendly community, explore custom biomes, and embark on epic quests.

Features

  • Custom terrain generation
  • Economy & shops
  • Player‑run towns
  • Monthly events & competitions
  • Active moderation & support
